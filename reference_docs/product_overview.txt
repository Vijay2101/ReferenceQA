NovaMed Health – Product Overview & Pricing
Document Version: 2.0 | Last Updated: March 2025

1. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
NovaMed Health is a cloud-based SaaS platform serving mid-size hospitals (50–500 beds) and outpatient clinics. The platform consists of four core modules:

Module 1 – Patient Intake Manager
Digitizes the patient check-in process. Patients receive a pre-visit link to complete intake forms on any device. Supports English and Spanish by default; additional languages available on request. Captures demographics, insurance information, consent forms, and chief complaint. Reduces average intake time from 18 minutes to under 5 minutes.

Module 2 – Insurance Eligibility Verifier
Performs real-time eligibility checks with 900+ payers via Availity and direct payer integrations. Returns benefit details, copay, deductible status, and prior authorisation requirements within 8 seconds. Reduces eligibility-related claim denials by an average of 43% (based on customer cohort data, FY2024).

Module 3 – Clinical Documentation Suite
AI-assisted clinical note generation using ambient voice capture. Supports SOAP notes, discharge summaries, and referral letters. Integrates with Epic, Cerner, and Athenahealth. Documentation is auto-coded with ICD-10 and CPT codes using our proprietary NovaMed ClinicalAI model.

Module 4 – Billing Workflow Manager
End-to-end claims management: charge capture, claim scrubbing, submission to payers, and remittance reconciliation. Integrated with Change Healthcare for clearinghouse services. Average first-pass claim acceptance rate across customer base: 96.2%.

3. FREE TRIAL AND ONBOARDING
NovaMed offers a 30-day free trial for Starter and Growth plans. No credit card required. Trial accounts include full feature access with a limit of 50 patient intake submissions. Dedicated onboarding is included for Growth and Enterprise customers. Average onboarding time: 14 days for Growth; 30–60 days for Enterprise.
