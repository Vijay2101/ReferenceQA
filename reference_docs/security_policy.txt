NovaMed Health – Information Security Policy
Document Version: 2.4 | Last Updated: March 2025

COMPANY OVERVIEW
NovaMed Health is a B2B SaaS platform for mid-size hospitals and outpatient clinics. Our platform enables healthcare providers to digitize patient intake, automate insurance eligibility verification, and manage clinical documentation through a unified cloud-based dashboard.

1. DATA ENCRYPTION
All customer data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption. Data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3. Database-level encryption is applied to all patient records stored in our HIPAA-compliant infrastructure. Encryption keys are managed using AWS KMS with automatic key rotation every 90 days. Backup data is encrypted with the same AES-256 standard and stored in geographically separate AWS regions.

2. AUTHENTICATION AND ACCESS CONTROL
NovaMed supports the following authentication methods:
- Username/password with bcrypt hashing (minimum 12 rounds)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via TOTP apps (e.g., Google Authenticator) or SMS OTP
- Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0 integration with identity providers such as Okta and Azure AD
- OAuth 2.0 for API access with short-lived tokens (15-minute expiry)

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) is enforced across all modules. There are four default roles: Admin, Clinician, Billing Staff, and Read-Only. Custom roles can be configured by enterprise accounts. Privileged access is reviewed quarterly.

3. VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT
The NovaMed security team conducts quarterly penetration testing with third-party vendors (most recently with Cobalt Security Labs in Q4 2024). Automated vulnerability scanning is performed weekly using Snyk for code dependencies and AWS Inspector for infrastructure. Critical vulnerabilities are patched within 72 hours of identification. High-severity findings are addressed within 7 days.

4. INCIDENT RESPONSE
NovaMed maintains a documented Incident Response Plan (IRP) following the NIST SP 800-61 framework. Incidents are classified into three tiers: P1 (Critical), P2 (High), P3 (Moderate). P1 incidents trigger immediate escalation to the CISO and executive team. Customer notification occurs within 72 hours of a confirmed breach, in compliance with HIPAA Breach Notification Rule. A post-incident review is conducted within 14 days.

5. COMPLIANCE AND CERTIFICATIONS
NovaMed Health is currently certified or compliant with:
- HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) – Business Associate Agreement (BAA) available for all enterprise customers
- SOC 2 Type II – Certified as of October 2024
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 – Certification in progress, expected Q3 2025
- HITRUST CSF v11 – Assessment completed November 2024

6. DATA RESIDENCY AND RETENTION
Customer data is stored exclusively in US-East-1 (Virginia) and US-West-2 (Oregon) AWS regions. EU customers can request EU data residency through our Enterprise tier. Data is retained for 7 years in accordance with HIPAA requirements, unless a shorter period is specified in the customer's BAA. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of contract termination.

7. SUBPROCESSORS AND THIRD PARTIES
NovaMed uses the following key subprocessors:
- Amazon Web Services (AWS) – Cloud hosting and infrastructure
- Twilio – SMS notifications for MFA and patient communication
- Stripe – Payment processing (PCI DSS Level 1 compliant)
- Mixpanel – Product analytics (anonymised data only, no PHI)
All subprocessors are reviewed annually and sign Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).

8. EMPLOYEE SECURITY
All employees undergo security awareness training upon onboarding and annually thereafter. Phishing simulation exercises are conducted quarterly. Background checks are completed for all employees with access to production systems. Access to PHI is on a need-to-know basis and logged for audit purposes.
