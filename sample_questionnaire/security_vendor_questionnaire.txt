NovaMed Health – Vendor Security & Compliance Questionnaire
Submitted by: Regional Health Network – Procurement Team
Date: March 2025

Please answer the following questions based on your current platform capabilities and security posture.

1. How is customer data encrypted at rest and in transit?

2. What authentication methods does your platform support, including MFA and SSO options?

3. Is your platform HIPAA compliant? Do you provide a Business Associate Agreement (BAA)?

4. What is your guaranteed uptime SLA for enterprise customers?

5. How frequently do you conduct penetration testing, and can you share the results?

6. What is your incident response process in the event of a data breach involving PHI?

7. What cloud infrastructure provider do you use, and in which geographic regions is data stored?

8. What EHR systems does your platform integrate with?

9. What are your Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO)?

10. Do you notify customers of new subprocessors, and how much notice is provided?

11. What certifications does your platform currently hold (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001, HITRUST)?

12. What data export formats do you support for portability?

13. What is your process for handling customer data upon contract termination?

14. Do you support custom data residency options for non-US customers?

15. What employee security controls are in place, including background checks and training?
